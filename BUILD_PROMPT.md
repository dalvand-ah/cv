**A few questions a reader might have**

*Why Synthetic Biology?*
It checks three boxes that matter to me at once: it's application-driven, it feels meaningful because it can directly improve people's lives, and it opens a career path outside academia. Most importantly, it's a field where I can actually watch a mathematical model turn into something real on the lab bench.

*How does this connect to your background in Category Theory?*
Directly, it doesn't. But there's a conceptual resonance I find compelling: Category Theory cares less about objects themselves than about the relationships between them and how they compose. In the same way, a complex biological system can be studied through the relationships between its parts, from a control-theory perspective — a lens I find genuinely interesting. Still, I see moving into this new field as a real challenge rather than a continuation of my past work, and it's a challenge I've chosen deliberately.

*Why biology, and not another applied field like financial mathematics?*
At heart, I'm drawn to application itself, not necessarily to biology specifically. Financial mathematics is applied too, but it didn't feel meaningful to me. Synthetic Biology offers both: real-world application and a sense of purpose.
